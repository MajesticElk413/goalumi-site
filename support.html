Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Goalumi keeps your habits, goals, history, and planning information locally on your device. It does not currently sync data between devices.

How can I back up my progress?

Open Goalumi → Settings → Back up my progress. Export the JSON file and save it somewhere you control, such as Files or your chosen cloud storage. The backup can contain personal goals and history and is not encrypted by Goalumi.

How do I restore a backup?

In Goalumi's Settings, choose the import/restore option, select a backup file, review the preview, and confirm only if you want to replace your device's current Goalumi data.

Why didn't I receive my reminder?

Check that the reminder is enabled in Goalumi and that Notifications are allowed for Goalumi in iPhone Settings. iOS may manage notification delivery according to Focus and other device settings.

What happens if I delete Goalumi?

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress. Save an exported backup before uninstalling or changing devices. We cannot recover data that was never backed up.

Does Goalumi require a paid subscription?

No. Goalumi 1.0 is free and has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Yes. Email us with a short description, the app version, your iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Please avoid sending private goal information or backup files unless you choose to and they are necessary.
