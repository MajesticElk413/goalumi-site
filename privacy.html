Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

App: Goalumi, an independent iOS habit and goal planner.
Developer and contact: Ioannis Valindras — goalumi.support@gmail.com.

Data controller: Ioannis Valindras. Postal contact details are provided in our Legal Notice. You can contact the controller at goalumi.support@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Goalumi stores your habits, completion history, long-term goals, milestone steps, saved schedules, planning preferences, and optional reminder settings in local application storage on your device. It uses this information to show your progress and provide planning features. No account is required.

2. Information we collect through the app

In this release, Goalumi does not operate a server that receives your habits or goals, and it does not contain an advertising SDK, developer-run analytics service, or cloud sync. We do not automatically receive your habits, planning information, or reminder settings. Standard iOS functions provide on-device notifications, file access, and sharing.

3. Backups and data portability

You may export a JSON backup using iOS sharing or Files. A backup can contain your goals, progress history, schedules, and preferences. Goalumi does not encrypt exported backups. You decide where to save or share them. If you save a backup with another company, such as a cloud-storage provider, that provider's terms and privacy practices apply.

When you import a backup, Goalumi displays a preview and requires confirmation before replacing current local app data. Temporary files may be created while sharing a backup.

4. Reminders

If enabled, reminders use iOS notification permission and are scheduled locally on your device. Goalumi does not use a remote push-notification server in this version. You can disable reminders in the app or your device's Settings.

5. External AI features

Goalumi can help prepare a prompt for you to copy to an external AI app or website. Goalumi does not send this information automatically. If you choose to paste it into a third-party service, that service's privacy policy applies. Only share information with third parties when you are comfortable doing so.

6. Support emails

If you contact goalumi.support@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use it to reply and investigate your request. This processing is generally based on our legitimate interest in responding to support and privacy enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or on legal obligations where applicable (Article 6(1)(c)). This email account is hosted by Google, which may process associated account and email data under its own privacy practices. We keep support correspondence while addressing the enquiry and for any longer period required to meet applicable legal obligations. We periodically review old correspondence for deletion. You may request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. This website

These public pages use no forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies set by Goalumi. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, as part of delivering the website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

8. Retention, control, and deletion

Local Goalumi data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, replace it with a backup, erase app storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling may remove locally stored information. Files previously exported or shared remain where you saved them until you delete them separately. We cannot remotely read, correct, or delete local data we never receive.

9. Children

Goalumi is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions.

10. Changes

We may update this policy if Goalumi's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page, together with its update date.

11. Contact and privacy requests

For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning information you have sent us, contact goalumi.support@gmail.com. You may also exercise applicable rights under data protection law or contact your competent data protection authority.
